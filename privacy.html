Hummingtide Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · effective 2 October 2026

Hummingtide is a music app run by Anujpal, an individual in India ("we", "us"). We decide how your personal data is used in Hummingtide, so we are the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your rights.

Hummingtide is only for people aged 18 or over (see "Children"). If you are 18 or over, you can listen without an account. Even then, our server sees your device's IP address, which we use only to stop abuse (see below). We collect more only when you sign in with Google.

Contact: palanuj259@gmail.com

We do not collect your contacts, location, microphone or files. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking. When the app shows your profile photo, your phone loads it directly from Google.

How we use it

  • To run your account: sign you in, show your name, photo and email, and keep your likes and playlists on every device.
  • To count plays: each counted play adds one to the song's play count, which decides what appears as "Popular". Repeat plays and floods are filtered out.
  • To keep the service safe: IP addresses, account IDs and logs help us block abuse and fix errors.

We use your data only for these purposes. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

When you sign in, you agree to us using your account data as this policy describes. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your account (see "Deleting your account"). Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before. We use IP addresses only to protect the service.

How long we keep it

  • Profile, likes and playlists: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in record at Supabase Auth (email, Google account ID, name, photo link): until you delete your account.
  • Plays: kept as anonymous records (song, seconds listened, time) with no link to you.
  • IP addresses in server memory: cleared when our server restarts or its memory table fills up. Never written to our database.
  • Server logs: each part of our server keeps at most 30 MB of logs. Older entries are overwritten automatically, and all logs are cleared when we update the server.
  • Backups: each encrypted backup is deleted after 30 days, so data you delete can stay in a backup for up to 30 days.

Security

  • Connections between the app, our server and our sign-in provider use HTTPS.
  • Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure storage. Hummingtide has no passwords; you sign in with Google.
  • Only our server, our encrypted-backup job and Hummingtide's owner can access the database. The owner adds music from their own computer and also has Supabase's dashboard. Supabase's public data interface can't read our database.
  • Backups are encrypted with AES-256 before upload.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we use it, and who we share it with;
  • correct, complete or update it (for example, your display name);
  • have it erased, or withdraw your consent (see "Deleting your account");
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to act;
  • complain to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

How: email palanuj259@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, subject "Privacy request". We may ask you to confirm it's you. We reply within 30 days. To nominate someone, email us their name and email address; you can change or cancel it any time.

Grievance Officer: Anujpal · palanuj259@gmail.com. We acknowledge complaints within 7 days and aim to resolve them within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This immediately deletes your Hummingtide profile, likes, playlists and your sign-in record at Supabase Auth, and signs you out.

Without the app: follow Delete your account, or email us from your sign-in address. We delete your whole account, including your sign-in record, within 30 days.

Deleted data can stay in our encrypted backups for up to 30 days. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. You can also remove Hummingtide's access to your Google account in your Google Account settings.

Children

Hummingtide is only for people aged 18 or over, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete the account and its data; parents or guardians can tell us at palanuj259@gmail.com. Some songs contain explicit lyrics and are marked "E".
