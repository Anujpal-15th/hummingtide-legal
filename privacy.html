Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Hummingtide is a music app run by Anujpal. This policy explains what we collect and why. You can listen without an account; we only collect personal data when you sign in.

We do not collect your contacts, location, microphone, or files. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

How we use it

  • To run your account: sign you in, show your name and photo, and keep your likes and playlists on every device.
  • To count plays: play counts decide what appears as "Popular". Repeat plays and floods are filtered out.
  • To keep the service safe: IP addresses and logs help us block abuse and fix errors.

We use your data only to provide Hummingtide. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. By signing in, you consent to this use; you can withdraw by deleting your account (see below).

How long we keep it

  • Account, likes and playlists: until you delete your account.
  • Play history: kept to count plays, as anonymous counts not linked to you.
  • Server logs: a few days to weeks; they are rotated automatically.
  • Backups: encrypted copies are deleted after 30 days.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure storage. Our database only accepts access through our own server, and backups are encrypted (AES-256). No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Email palanuj259@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We reply within 30 days.

To delete your account, use Profile → Delete account in the app, or see Delete your account.

Children

Hummingtide is not for children under 13. Some songs have explicit lyrics, marked with an "E". If you are under 18, use Hummingtide with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the date at the top. Questions or complaints: palanuj259@gmail.com.
