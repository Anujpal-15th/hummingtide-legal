Delete your Hummingtide account

Last updated: 2 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Hummingtide and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out and the deletion happens straight away.

By email (if you can't use the app)

Email palanuj259@gmail.com from the Google address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name and photo link)
  • Your liked songs and playlists
  • Your sign-in record (email and Google identity)

What is kept

  • Play counts, only as anonymous numbers that are not linked to you.
  • Encrypted backups may still contain your data for up to 30 days, then they are deleted.
